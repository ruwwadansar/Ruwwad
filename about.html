OUR INSTITUTION

Ansar Arabic College.

Valavannur, Malappuram, Kerala.

More Than a Union.
A Movement.

RUWWAD is the democratically elected Student's Union of Ansar Arabic College for the academic year 2026-27. We are not just a governing body; we are a platform dedicated to the welfare, growth, and development of every student on campus.

Our name, "RUWWAD," means pioneers and reflects our philosophy: leadership, ambition, and progress. We strive to create an inclusive atmosphere where art, sports, and academics flourish side by side. We aim to be the heartbeat of campus life.

Our College.

Ansar Arabic College Valavannur is situated at the Valavannur Village in the Tirur Taluk of the backward district of Malappuram in Kerala State. The College was established in the year 1964 by a reformist socio-cultural educational organization called 'Ansarullah Sangham'.

The college provides a unique blend of traditional Arabic studies and modern academic disciplines, fostering an environment where cultural heritage meets contemporary education. The Student's Union works hand-in-hand with the management to ensure the student community's voice is heard and valued.
